Graph Neural Networks · PhD

Graph neural networks for composition–rheology–printability relationships in DIW ceramic inks

A. Bejerano, C. Ramírez, M. Belmonte · ICV-CSIC

Oral presentation · LX Congress of the Spanish Ceramic and Glass Society (SECV), Córdoba 2026

Heterogeneous graph model (HeteroConv, relation-specific message passing) trained on formulations extracted from the literature. Printability separates in the rheological plane (yield stress τy vs. G′) rather than in composition space, revealing two failure modes — insufficient yield and excessive stiffness — with a threshold consistent with the ≈200 Pa reported in the literature.
